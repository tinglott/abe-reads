# Privacy Policy — Abe Reads

**Effective date:** 2026

Abe Reads is a reading-comprehension app for children, rebuilt from a 1999
educational program. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect

**None.** Abe Reads does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information such as names, email addresses, locations, or contacts.

- **Reading progress** (pages read, quiz scores, stars) is stored **on the
  device only** by default.
- **No account** is required to use the app.

## Optional cloud features

The app works fully offline. Two optional cloud features, if you choose to
configure them, behave as follows:

- **Progress sync (Supabase):** when configured, progress is associated with an
  **anonymous** identifier (no email or name). Nothing else is sent.
- **AI bonus questions (Hugging Face):** when configured, the story text is
  sent to generate extra questions. No personal data is included.
- **Read-aloud voices (Microsoft Edge):** the text you ask to be read is sent to
  Microsoft's speech service to produce audio. It is not stored or linked to you.

All three are opt-in and off unless you add API keys.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for young readers. It contains no ads, no analytics
trackers, no in-app purchases, and no links to external services that collect
data. If a parent or school enables optional cloud sync, only anonymous progress
is transmitted.

## Your control

Because all progress lives on your device, deleting the app erases it. No
account to close.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository.
